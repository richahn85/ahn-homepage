Customer Management

Hyundai Department Store CRM

From customer satisfaction surveys to CRM consulting and follow-on projects.

CONTEXT

Project context

A project delivering customer satisfaction surveys and CRM consulting for Hyundai Department Store.

CHALLENGE

The core challenge

The heart of the project was treating customer research and customer relationship management as a single management challenge.

MY ROLE

My role

Project manager (PM) for CRM consulting. I won the customer satisfaction survey engagement and led delivery of the CRM project.

WHAT WE DID

What we did

We won Hyundai Department Store’s customer satisfaction survey, and I joined the CRM consulting engagement as project manager. Follow-on projects continued for two more years.

IMPACT

Why it mattered

The work did not stop at a satisfaction survey; it grew into CRM consulting and two years of follow-on engagements.

WHAT IT MEANS TODAY

Lessons for management today

The value of customer data is not determined by volume alone. In today’s use of AI, too, what matters is connecting an understanding of customers to the organization’s judgment and execution.

Source
